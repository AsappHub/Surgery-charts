Acute Appendicitis

What is appendicitis?

Appendicitis is a condition of painful swelling caused by inflammation of the vermiform appendix.

Symptoms

  • Stomach pain on the lower right side
  • Fever
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Loss of appetite
  • Bloating
  • Constipation or diarrhoea
  • Painful urination

Signs and triads

1. Two triads

Each triad is a group of three findings.

Dieulafoy triad
  • Hyperesthesia (extra sensitivity) in the skin over the area.
  • Point tenderness.
  • Muscle guarding.
Murphy’s triad
  • Pain in the right lower abdomen (right lower quadrant).
  • Nausea and vomiting.
  • Fever.

2. Four signs

Tests done at the bedside. Each one causes pain in the right lower abdomen.

Rovsing sign

Pain in the right lower abdomen when the left lower abdomen is pressed.

Obturator sign

Pain in the right lower abdomen when the bent right hip is rotated inwards and outwards.

Psoas sign

Pain in the right lower abdomen when the right hip is extended, or when the right hip is bent against resistance.

Dunphy sign

Sharp pain in the right lower abdomen when the patient coughs voluntarily.

McBurney’s point

A point on the abdominal wall between the navel and the right anterior superior iliac spine. Pressure here causes the most pain in acute appendicitis.

Evaluation by surgeon

The surgeon takes the history, examines the patient and orders investigations. This decides whether the appendicitis is acute or complicated.

Acute appendicitis

An inflamed appendix without complications. The surgeon chooses between conservative treatment and an operation.

Complicated appendicitis

Includes appendicular mass, suppurative appendicitis and perforated appendicitis. Here too the surgeon chooses between conservative treatment and an operation.

Conservative treatment

Treatment without surgery. If it is successful, the patient goes home with antibiotics. If it fails, the patient goes for operation.

Operation

Surgery to remove the appendix (appendicectomy). It is chosen directly by the surgeon, or when conservative treatment has failed.

Home

After successful conservative treatment, the patient is discharged home with antibiotics.
